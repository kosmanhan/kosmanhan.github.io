Robinhood Sweepstakes

Written on: December 31, 2025

Robinhood had an end of year sweepstakes in which ~$6 million was awarded to members who had to be at a landing page on their mobile app. This occurred over six days at 8:30 EST.

How many gold memberships (annual fee of $50) would it take for Robinhood to fund this sweepstakes? 6000000/50=120000 members.

The interesting part in this was how they had a pool of crypto that was divided amongst the members that were present on that day. As we know the amount on offer, we can figure out from it how many people there were, by knowing how much 1 person got. I happened to be present on all days, so I'll take the total amount and divide it with mine to find the number of people:

500,000 1.19 420,168
750,000 2.8 267,857
850,000 2.56 332,031
1,000,000 2.74 364,964
1,000,000 2.01 497,512
1,500,000 2.51 300,000
